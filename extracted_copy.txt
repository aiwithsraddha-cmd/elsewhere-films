Block 2 · Vision & Mission (one statement band)
Vision line (large)
A future where South Asian stories are discovered, celebrated, and sustained across the world - by design, not by chance.
Mission paragraph
We work with South Asian independent cinema  -  on visibility, circulation, and pathways. We help films reach the right festivals, markets,  and  distributors,   combining  our  pathway intelligence  model  with the judgment of experienced industry practitioners.

Block 3 · Why We Exist
Opening
Some strong films travel the world. Others, just as good, disappear after their first screening.
Body
Why? 
It c ould  be the wrong premiere, no one championing it in the right rooms, no reason for the press to take notice, or simply no plan for what comes after the first festival.
Unfortunately, the biggest price is paid by films with the least room for error - a first film, tight budgets and no banner behind it.
An entire ecosystem exists to help films get made. But what about help for finished films to find their way into the world?
Elsewhere  was built for that gap - drawing on hundreds of film journeys and the experience of people who have spent their careers opening the doors that matter.

Block 4 · What We Do
Intro line
Our work begins when your film is finished, and follows it through festivals, markets and distribution.
Offerings
Festival Scheduling & Entries
A festival calendar built around the film  -  its profile, timeline and eligibility. Premiere rules, windows and submission costs mapped before anything is filed. We take care of the entries, too.
Full Pathway Recommendation A clear route from premiere to markets, distribution and audiences - with scenarios, opportunities and the industry connections that can help move the film forward.
Positioning & Packaging The words and materials that travel with the film: loglines, synopses, director’s statements, decks and buyer-facing materials. Everything aligned, so the film is presented clearly and consistently wherever it goes .
Distribution & Audience Finding the right sales agents, distributors, platforms and buyers - then supporting the introductions and conversations that follow. Looking beyond the home market to diaspora, cinephile and crossover audiences where the film can travel .
Co-production We also co-produce films aligned with our vision  - involved in development, international positioning and shaping the film’s route into the world.

Block 1 · Journey
Headline
Where is your film on its journey?
Card 1
Nearing Completion The locked cut is in sight. This is the moment to make the decisions that shape what comes next - premiere strategy, eligibility, timing and the right route for the film.
Card 2
Festival Ready The film is done. Now come the bigger questions: which premiere, in what sequence, and toward which rooms? Or whether the festival route is the right one at all!
Card 3
In Circulation
The premiere is behind you. Now it’s about building momentum - markets, distributors, new audiences - and giving the film a life beyond its first screening.
